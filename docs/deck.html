Problem

The fastest-growing UPI frauds target the payer, not the system. Each one leaves a signal that is visible at the moment of payment.

Scam typeHow it worksSignal available during the payment
Digital arrestImpersonators posing as police, CBI or customs keep the victim on a call and direct transfers to a "verification" account.Payer on an active call; first payment to the receiver; amount far above the payer's norm.
Collect-request fraudThe victim is told to approve a request to "receive" a prize or refund. Approving a COLLECT request debits the account.COLLECT from an unknown receiver; recently opened receiving account.
Account takeoverAfter a SIM swap or remote-access install, the attacker drains the account from a new device.Unrecognised device and location; burst of payments to new receivers.
Money mulesPurchased or rented accounts collect from many victims and forward funds within minutes.Many distinct payers to one receiver within a day; rapid onward transfers.

UPI processes more than 10 billion payments a month. Once a payment settles, recovery depends on slow freeze-and-refund processes. Prevention has to happen inside the authorisation window.

Sources: NPCI UPI product statistics; RBI and Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) public advisories on digital-arrest and collect-request fraud.
Kavach2
Design requirements

An effective control must decide within the payment, explain its decision and never expose customer identity.

Inline and fast

The score must return inside the authorisation call, in milliseconds, for every payment. Batch or post-settlement review cannot stop a transfer that has already settled.

Explainable

A customer who is warned needs a reason specific to their situation. An analyst who releases a held payment needs the evidence. A regulator needs an audit trail.

Private by design

Behavioural profiling must not create a new store of identifiable payment data. Identifiers are tokenised before any processing, and demographics are excluded from the model.

Kavach meets all three requirements in a single service on IBM LinuxONE, next to the systems of record that already process the payment.

Solution

Each payment receives one of three graded decisions with plain-language reasons, so friction is applied only where risk is real.

Architecture

Scoring runs on LinuxONE inside the authorisation path. Identifiers are tokenised before any feature or model sees them.

Kavach5
Method

Behavioural features computed per payment feed two complementary models, evaluated strictly on future data.

Features

14 signals: amount relative to the payer's norm, first-time receiver, device and location, live-call flag, COLLECT type, payment bursts, 24-hour spend, receiver fan-in and account age. A receiver, device or city becomes trusted only after 24 hours.

Models

Gradient-boosted trees learn known fraud patterns. An isolation forest trained only on genuine traffic flags unfamiliar behaviour for step-up, giving coverage beyond the patterns in the training labels.

Explanation

Occlusion attribution: each signal is reset to a typical genuine value and the drop in risk is measured. This runs in one batched model call, and the top signals become reason codes.

Evaluation

Time-based split: train on days 1–17, set thresholds on days 18–22, report on days 23–30. The same feature code is used in training replay and live serving, so there is no train/serve skew.

Data. 139,755 simulated UPI payments over 30 days (4,000 customers, 600 merchants, 80 mule accounts). It includes four fraud patterns from public advisories and deliberately includes hard cases: large payments to new legitimate receivers, genuine payments made during calls, new merchants, travel, device changes, and long-established accounts used as mules. Real UPI data is not publicly available.

Impact and roadmap

Kavach protects the customers most exposed to payment fraud and gives banks an auditable, low-friction control.

Beneficiaries

  • Elderly and first-time digital payment users
  • Banks and payment service providers: fewer losses, disputes and refunds
  • Law enforcement: earlier identification of mule accounts

Next three months

  • Serve the model on the Telum accelerator
  • Move tokenisation keys into Crypto Express
  • Deploy as a Secure Execution guest
  • Pilot on anonymised bank data

Beyond

  • Privacy-preserving mule-account signals shared across banks
  • Additional Indian languages for customer messages
  • Continuous drift and fairness monitoring
