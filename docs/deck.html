The problem

UPI made payments instant. It made scams instant too.

"Digital arrest"

Fake police / CBI keep a victim, often elderly, on a call until they transfer their savings.

Collect-request scam

"Approve to receive your ₹4,999 prize." Approving actually sends money.

SIM-swap takeover

New phone, other city: the account is drained in minutes.

Money mules

Rented accounts collect from many victims and pass the money on within minutes.

10+ billion UPI payments a month. These scams beat people, not encryption.

Once a payment settles, recovery is slow and often impossible. The only moment to stop it is during the transaction.

Every payment gets a decision, and a reason, in ~13 ms

How the AI works

Behaviour, not blacklists

Streaming feature store

14 live signals per payment: amount vs usual, trusted payees / devices / cities (trust takes 24 h), bursts, 24 h spend, on-call, COLLECT, receiver fan-in. Same code trains and serves, so there is no skew.

Two models

Gradient-boosted trees learn known scam patterns. An Isolation Forest trained only on genuine traffic flags never-seen-before behaviour for a STEP-UP.

Explainer + policy

Occlusion attribution (one batched call) ranks the signals behind each alert. Thresholds tuned for ≥90% HOLD precision and ~97% warning recall.

Data: 140k simulated UPI payments (4,000 customers, 600 merchants, 80 mules, 30 days) with 4 fraud patterns from public NPCI / RBI / I4C advisories, plus hard negatives (rent to new payees, calls, travel, new shops, old rented mule accounts). Time-split: train on weeks 1–2, tune on days 18–22, test on the final unseen week.

Responsible AI · security by design

Useful AI without exposing anyone

  • Identity never exposed: UPI and device IDs are HMAC-tokenized at ingestion. The model, features and logs never see them. Analysts see masks only.
  • No demographics in the model. Age is used only to audit outcomes.
  • Explainable by default: no alert without its reasons.
  • Human in the loop: HOLD pauses the payment, it doesn't cancel it.
